e-means in DuckDB-WASM

The emeans DuckDB extension, compiled to WebAssembly and loaded into DuckDB-WASM, clustering in this browser. e-means is mini-batch k-means whose centroids follow an exponential moving average of their batch means, with the batch size and step from fitted scaling laws (Bessa et al. 2026).

Embedding-sized vectors

  
Real embeddings have hundreds or thousands of dimensions. This builds n vectors of dimension d around 16
    centres inside DuckDB and clusters them, timing both steps. DuckDB-WASM runs on one thread without the
    native build's Accelerate or threads, so the native extension is many times faster.
