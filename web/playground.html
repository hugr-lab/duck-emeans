The e-means playground

Lloyd's k-means and e-means side by side on 2-D data, both run by the emeans DuckDB extension inside DuckDB-WASM in this browser, then replayed. In the style of the playground by the authors of e-means (Bessa et al. 2026), with the computation in SQL. A simpler demo is also available.

Everything runs locally; no data leaves the page. Built with Scala.js and Laminar. Source: github.com/hugr-lab/duck-emeans.
